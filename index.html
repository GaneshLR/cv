Onboarding journey

I onboarded customers end to end. Every new hotel brand at Cendyn went through the same six stages, and I owned all of it — the technical work and the client relationship.

  1. Discovery

    Requirement calls with hotel marketing and IT; campaign, data and integration needs turned into a documented technical design and delivery plan.

  2. Data integration

    PMS guest-data feeds from Oracle OPERA, Mews and SynXis into the CRM, ready for segmentation.

  3. Sender authentication

    SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and verified, with inbox-placement checks before the first send.

  4. Platform configuration

    Marketing Cloud, CRM, loyalty and CMS configured; templates, content blocks and segments built.

  5. Training & UAT

    Client teams trained; rendering, links, personalisation and data integrity checked to UAT sign-off.

  6. Go-live & support

    Release readiness and launch, then the escalation point for technical, integration and deliverability issues.
